About Us

The CADRE Alumni Organization was officially formed in 2026 to connect and support the alumni and future alumni of the Digital Media Art program and CADRE Laboratory for New Media at San José State University.

CADRE (Computers in Art, Design, Research, and Education) faculty, students and alumni have participated in the evolution of art and technology for 40 years. Together with the CADRE Student Organization, the CADRE Alumni Organization seeks to celebrate CADRE's legacy, illuminate its future, and continue exploring digital media art together.
